Copy not free

Problem type https://mtgscan.reilley.dev/problems/copy-not-free of the mtgscan MCP connection

What it means

The incoming copy is not a free copy. It is in a deck, or another planned swap holds it. Nothing was saved.

What to do next

Find a free copy with search_collection, with rows set to copies and is_free set to true. To move a copy from another deck, recommend the move in the chat. The user makes it.
