Free copy exists

Problem type https://mtgscan.reilley.dev/problems/free-copy-exists of the mtgscan MCP connection

What it means

The user has a free copy of the card that is not protected, so there is nothing to buy. The copy_ids member names the free copies. Nothing was saved.

What to do next

Make a planned swap with one of those copies with create_planned_swaps.
