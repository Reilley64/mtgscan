Outgoing card not in deck

Problem type https://mtgscan.reilley.dev/problems/outgoing-not-in-deck of the mtgscan MCP connection

What it means

The outgoing card of a deck suggestion is not in the deck. Either the outgoing copy is not one of the deck's cards, or the deck has none of the outgoing basic land card. Nothing was saved.

What to do next

Read the deck with get_deck. Name a copy_id from its cards, or a basic land card that it has.
