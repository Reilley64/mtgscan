Invalid card

Problem type https://mtgscan.reilley.dev/problems/invalid-card of the mtgscan MCP connection

What it means

The oracle_id of the incoming card is not a card in the card catalog, or it is a basic land card. Basic lands are never bought. Nothing was saved.

What to do next

Find the card with search_catalog, and send its oracle_id. Never add a basic land to the buylist.
