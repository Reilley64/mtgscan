Duplicate open

Problem type https://mtgscan.reilley.dev/problems/duplicate-open of the mtgscan MCP connection

What it means

An open planned swap or buylist card in the deck already has the same incoming card, as_commander, and outgoing card. The planned_swap_id or buylist_card_id member names it. Nothing was saved.

What to do next

Read it with list_planned_swaps or list_buylist. Do not make the same planned swap or buylist card again.
