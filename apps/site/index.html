Scan your Magic cards. Build Commander decks.

mtgscan is a phone app for Magic: The Gathering players. It scans your cards, keeps your collection, and helps you build Commander decks from the cards you own.

In development for iPhone. Not on the App Store yet.

  • Scan cards one at a time

    Point the camera at one card. mtgscan recognizes it on your phone and adds it to a scan batch. Then you add the batch to your collection.

  • Keep your collection

    See every card you own and what it is worth. Protect copies to keep them out of your decks.

  • Build Commander decks

    Build decks from the copies you own. See deck validity and an estimated bracket, and revert any deck change.

  • Get deck suggestions from your AI chat

    Connect a compatible AI chat client through the MCP connection. It can read your cards and decks and write deck suggestions with a reason. You decide which ones go into your decks.

Sign in with Apple or Google. Your collection and decks are private to your account.
